# Founding Canon v2 acceptance rubric

Every child is compared with its named v1 parent. A child is promotable only when all required checks pass.

| Check | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Reference continuity | The parent's composition family, palette, atmosphere, scale, and strongest visual idea remain recognizable. |
| New iteration | The child is a materially new frame rather than a near pixel-copy. |
| Subject anatomy | Required unit count, limb count, chassis type, and formation are mechanically plausible and match the subject correction. |
| Spatial truth | Required lanes, bounded regions, targets, clusters, or emissions are present and countable where the prompt specifies them. |
| Mechanic legibility | A reviewer can name the depicted mechanic without relying on a caption. |
| Text hygiene | No readable text, pseudo-text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, or branded insignia. |
| Originality | No recognizable external franchise design language. |

Calibration chooses the lowest Redux reference strength that preserves continuity while still permitting the requested semantic correction. The production manifest records the selected strength and complete parent lineage per image.

## Calibration decision

The five-family contact review selected **0.35** for production. It preserved each parent's palette, atmosphere, scale, and composition family while producing visibly more camera and silhouette freedom than 0.65 or 1.0. Higher strengths were valid variations but too often converged on the source pose and layout, especially for the commander and mech exemplars.
