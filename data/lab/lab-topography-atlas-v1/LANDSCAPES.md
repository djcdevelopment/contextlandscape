# Evidence-driven lab landscapes

This deterministic package classifies **53 labs**, maps **6,400 commanders**, reconciles **1,231,503 artillery fires**, and derives round/coordinate playback from **720,000 desperation records**.

- Commander Field height is an ability rate per commander appearance.
- Artillery Relief height defaults to shells per 1,000 player-runs.
- Desperation Theatre full-cohort action and outcome rates use all eligible opportunities.
- Spatial coordinates and exemplars use the campaign's documented 1-in-64 trace sample (`seed % 64 === 0`); trace coverage is displayed in the viewer.
- Outcome and causal-effect colors remain distinct from usage elevation.
- Individual views are deterministic artillery-decision exemplars, not complete unit-action replays.
