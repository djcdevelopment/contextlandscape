The attention economy

Your fleet produces more work than you can inspect. Looking costs attention; deciding is free. Anything you haven't reviewed when the round closes is accepted unseen.

So the question is never which mech is reliable — they're all equally often right. It's what you choose not to look at, and whose confidence you believe when you do.
